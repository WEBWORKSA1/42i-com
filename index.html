Questions, answered

What is 42i?

42i is an independent AI discovery hub. We curate the most useful AI tools, prompts and tutorials, and connect businesses with vetted AI experts. The name nods to the famous “answer to everything” — plus “i” for intelligence.

Is 42i free?

Yes. Browsing, saving, comparing tools, prompts and guides are free forever. We earn through clearly labelled sponsorships, advertising and affiliate links.

How do you choose which tools to list?

Every submission is reviewed by an editor for usefulness, transparency and real-world traction. Paid listings get faster review and more visibility, never a better verdict.

Can you help my business implement AI?

Yes — request a free AI Action Plan on our AI Solutions page and we’ll recommend tools and, if you want, match you with a vetted expert.

How do I list my AI tool?

Use the Submit page. Free listings join the review queue; Fast-Track and Featured plans go live in 24–48 hours.
